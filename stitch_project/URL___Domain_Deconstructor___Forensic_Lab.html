Inspecting: 03 Apex Domain (account-verification-portal)

The Apex domain is the actual entity purchased and managed by the registrant. Regardless of how many legitimate corporate names precede it in the subdomain string (e.g., "microsoftonline.com"), network routing resolves exclusively to the authoritative nameservers of account-verification-portal.net.

Forensic Reading Heuristic

The Golden Rule of Domain Reading

Look immediately to the left of the first single forward slash (/) following the scheme. The apex domain is strictly composed of the two rightmost dot-separated labels prior to that slash. Everything situated to the left of these two labels is purely user-configurable subdomain space.
